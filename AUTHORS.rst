Upstream: DSSG triage
---------------------

triage-pg is a rewrite of `DSSG triage <https://github.com/dssg/triage>`_. It
keeps that project's pipeline shape and its ``timechop`` temporal-splitting
component; everything else was rebuilt. The project was originated and led by
**Rayid Ghani**, started at the Center for Data Science and Public Policy,
University of Chicago, and later maintained at Carnegie Mellon University.

How to cite
-----------

``CITATION.cff`` is the citation of record for triage-pg and carries the
upstream project as a ``references`` entry; ``.zenodo.json`` carries the same
credit as Zenodo ``contributors`` and links the two works with an
``isDerivedFrom`` relation on the DOI record.
